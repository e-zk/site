Backend

This website is a collection of static HTML pages generated by a custom site generator. It is a VM on “someone else’s computer” running OpenBSD. The pages are served by relayd, and httpd. The entire content of the site can be found in a git repository.

A hidden service mirror of this site is available and can be accessed at the following onion address: 64wv2uqwjacqer7z5d6ooqgrvjwlioizmo7hgmxm7zxerbvgnoqhafid.onion.

Writing

Posts are edited in the vis editor as Markdown, or more recently, djot files. In the footer of most pages, you can open the plaintext representation. I try to keep the text width under 80 characters for readability.
I sometimes run longer posts through aspell to check spelling, but not always.

Design

I handwrite the CSS here. I do enjoy playing with CSS so you’ll find the layout, style, fonts, colours for this site will change pretty often. I muck about with CSS more than I actually write content for the site.

Typography

As of writing, this site is using your defaults for ‘serif’, and ‘monospace’ fonts. I think this is sufficient for readability; seems to look okay-ish on most system’s defaults. I’m often torn between importing nicer looking fonts and also maintaining the simplicity and “lightweight”-ness of the site.

JavaScript

I don’t use any JavaScript on the site.

Tracking

I don’t do any analytics or tracking for your visits, but I do have access logs for the site somewhere around here. I’ve never had to dig into them. Now I’m curious what they look like…

AI

I don’t use any AI/LLMs to generate the code, or content on this site.
