The Capture

I finished watching The Capture, season 2 recently. (It’s free to watch on iView if you’re in Australia).

Without spoiling too much, it’s a fantastic UK conspiracy/cop/spy thriller about weaponised deepfakes and their effect on society—particularly the justice system. The show covers their sinister use for political gain, and in corporate and intelligence espionage.

It is not a true story, but it’s becoming more and more true to life as AI generated imagery, and is the focus of the show, live camera feed manipulation technology continues to improve rapidly.

It’s a horrifying prediction of the world we are slowly inching towards day by day. Where we can’t trust anything that we see, video evidence is thrown out of the courts completely and the intricacies of intelligence, espionage, terrorism and politics are changed forever.

There is a third season—but I think season 2 wrapped it up quite nicely.
